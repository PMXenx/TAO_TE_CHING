## ✅ BEST PRACTICES

### DO:
✅ Personalize when possible
✅ Mention it's free and ad-free
✅ Offer installation help
✅ Share your own experience with it
✅ Keep messages concise
✅ Include both link and QR code options
✅ Follow up if appropriate

❌ Spam people with repeated messages
❌ Share without context
❌ Oversell or exaggerate benefits
❌ Make it seem complicated
❌ Forget to mention iOS/Safari requirement
❌ Share broken or wrong links
❌ Pressure people to install
