## 🎬 Recording Tips

### For Both Videos

**Before Recording:**
- Clean up your home screen (remove sensitive apps/widgets)
- Turn on "Do Not Disturb" (no notifications during recording)
- Charge your phone to 100% (battery indicator looks better)
- Turn off auto-brightness (consistent lighting)
- Use WiFi (faster loading)

**Screen Recording Settings:**
- Enable "Record audio" if you're doing voiceover live
- OR record video silently and add voiceover later
- Record in highest quality available
- Hold phone vertically (portrait mode)

**During Recording:**
- Speak slowly and clearly
- Pause for 2 seconds between major steps
- Keep your finger visible when tapping (helps viewers see what to tap)
- If you make a mistake, pause for 5 seconds then restart that section (easy to edit out)

**After Recording:**
- Trim the beginning and end
- Add text overlays for the URL (makes it easier to read)
- Add background music (optional, keep it soft)
- Export in MP4 format for best compatibility

## 🌐 Multi-Language Considerations

If your clients speak other languages, you can:

1. **Record silent video** with on-screen text only
2. **Add subtitles** in multiple languages
3. **Create separate audio tracks** for different languages
4. **Use universal icons** and visual cues

Most video editing software (iMovie, CapCut, DaVinci Resolve) supports subtitles.
