## ❓ TROUBLESHOOTING

**iPhone: "I can't find the Share button"**
- It's at the BOTTOM of Safari (looks like 📤)
- Try rotating your phone if you can't see it

**iPhone: "I don't see Add to Home Screen"**
- Make sure you SCROLLED DOWN in the share menu
- It's in the second or third row

**iPhone: "It says I must use Safari"**
- Close Chrome/Firefox completely
- Open Safari (blue compass icon)
- Try again

**Android: "No install banner appears"**
- Make sure you're using Chrome browser
- Wait 5 seconds after page loads
- Try menu (⋮) → "Add to Home screen"

**App won't work offline?**
- Browse 2-3 passages when online first
- Wait 30 seconds after first visit
- Close and reopen the app

## 📖 ABOUT THE TAO TE CHING

The Tao Te Ching is an ancient Chinese text written around 400 BCE, attributed to the philosopher Laozi. It contains 81 short chapters offering wisdom about living in harmony with the Tao (the Way).

This app makes these timeless teachings accessible on your phone, with:
- Modern English translations
- Practical daily applications
- Beautiful visual presentation
