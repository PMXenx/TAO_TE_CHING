# 📱 iOS Installation Guide - Tao Te Ching App

**Simple step-by-step instructions for installing the Tao Te Ching app on iPhone or iPad**

---

## ⚠️ Important: Use Safari Browser

**You MUST use Safari browser on iOS** - Chrome, Firefox, and other browsers cannot install web apps on iPhone.

---

## 📋 Installation Steps (5 Simple Steps)

### Step 1: Open Safari

1. Tap the **Safari** icon on your iPhone (blue compass icon)
2. If Safari opens to a different page, that's okay

---

### Step 2: Visit the App Website

1. Tap the **address bar** at the top of Safari
2. Type or paste this link:
   ```
   https://pmxenx.github.io/tao-te-ching-app/index.html
   ```
3. Tap **Go** on your keyboard
4. Wait for the page to load (you'll see a gold "TAO TE CHING" header)

---

### Step 3: Tap the Share Button

1. Look at the **bottom** of your Safari window
2. Find the **Share button** - it looks like a square with an arrow pointing up: 📤
3. Tap the Share button
4. A menu will appear from the bottom

---

### Step 4: Add to Home Screen

1. **Scroll down** in the share menu
2. Look for the option **"Add to Home Screen"**
   - It has a icon that looks like a phone with a plus sign: ➕
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. A preview screen will appear

---

### Step 5: Confirm Installation

1. You'll see the app name: **"Tao Daily"**
2. You'll see the app icon (purple mystical design)
3. Tap the blue **"Add"** button in the top right corner
4. **Done!** The app is now on your home screen

---

## ✅ Using the Installed App

1. **Go to your iPhone home screen**
2. **Find the "Tao Daily" icon** (look for the purple icon)
3. **Tap to open** - the app opens in fullscreen mode
4. Enjoy your daily Tao wisdom!

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### "I can't find the Share button"

- The Share button is at the **bottom** of Safari (not the top)
- On iPad, it might be at the **top** instead
- It looks like this: 📤 (square with arrow pointing up)
- If you still can't find it, try rotating your phone

### "I don't see 'Add to Home Screen' in the menu"

- Make sure you **scrolled down** in the share menu
- It's usually in the second or third row of options
- Look for the phone icon with a plus sign: ➕
- Try closing and reopening the share menu

### "It says I must use Safari"

- You might be using Chrome, Firefox, or another browser
- Close that browser completely
- Open the **Safari** app (blue compass icon)
- Try again from Step 2

### "The app won't open offline"

- Make sure you browsed a few passages when online first
- Close the app completely and reopen it
- Wait 30 seconds after first installation for caching to complete

### "I accidentally deleted the app"

- No problem! Just follow these steps again
- Your progress is not saved when you delete the app
- Reinstallation is quick (takes about 1 minute)

3. **Try closing Safari completely** and starting over
4. **Restart your iPhone** if nothing works
